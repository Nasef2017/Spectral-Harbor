# References and data attribution

Accessed/verified 2026-10-27. Cite satellite products, relevant methods and software separately. Product license and attribution requirements are independent of the plugin code license. Save `provenance.json` and the original `*_Processing_Metadata.json` with derived results. The former records the run/scene IDs and engine; the latter records upstream scientific metadata and, for single scenes, per-band normalization details. No DOI is claimed for this plugin.

1. Copernicus / ESA. **Collection 1 Level-2A**. Twelve surface-reflectance spectral bands, with B10 excluded; Sen2Cor scene classification. https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/sentinel-data-access/sentinel-products/sentinel-2-data-products/collection-1-level-2a . Sentinel data legal notice: https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice . Use the appropriate “Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [year]” attribution for derived products and preserve product notices.
2. USGS. **Landsat Collection 2 Surface Reflectance**. Seven OLI SR bands and scaling. https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-reflectance . Collection 2 product DOI: https://doi.org/10.5066/P9OGBGM6 . Acknowledge USGS/NASA Landsat data and the actual scene acquisition dates; consult https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-data-access for access terms.
3. Element 84. **Earth Search STAC API**, catalog and collections. https://github.com/Element84/earth-search and https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1 . Collection metadata https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-c1-l2a and https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/landsat-c2-l2 . Apply each asset's raster metadata rather than assuming offsets from a different collection. Historical offset discussion: https://github.com/Element84/earth-search/discussions/26 . Microsoft Planetary Computer SAS signing: https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/docs/concepts/sas/ . These are data access services, not the source of the physical satellite measurement.

6. Xu, H. (2006). Modification of normalised difference water index (NDWI) to enhance open water features in remotely sensed imagery. *International Journal of Remote Sensing*, 27(14), 3025–3033. https://doi.org/10.1080/01431160600589179 .
